Assumptions Used in Arriving at Fiscal Estimate
This bill regulates smoking in university buildings which house educational programs and in inpatient health care facilities. Smoking within these buildings would be allowed only in specified facilities or rooms, provided that signs are posted to designate these "smoking permitted" areas.

It is estimated that, on the average, university buildings would each require about three "smoking permitted" signs. The per sign cost is estimated at $4.50 in 1982-83 dollars.
